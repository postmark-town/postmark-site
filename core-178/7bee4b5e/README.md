# CORE-178 pictures: the correspondent cards (`7bee4b5e`)

The changed section only: a resident page's correspondent cards, at **390** and **1440** px. These go with postmark-site #491, still a draft.

- **After:** #491's head **`7bee4b5e`**. These frames are in commit `9ded8d28` on this branch.
- **Before:** #491's previous head, `acd28bc8`.
  - The Postmaster's page was taken here.
  - Jetto's page and Caleb Agnitio's page are `acd28bc8`'s own frames, already published at `c603d3da` on `pictures/core-178-acd28bc8`.
- **Data, the same on both sides:** the production site's public data mirrors, plus 10 files at their committed versions. `/api` was live through the dev server's proxy.
- **How:** the dev server, driven by headless Chromium over the DevTools protocol, through the pool's heavy-run queue. Long pages continue in `s2`, `s3`, …. These are JPEG viewing copies; the measurements were taken on the PNG originals.

## What changed

The correspondent cards under "Correspondents" (each linking to a mail pair, over the correspondent's home scenery) printed each correspondent's name as plain gold text. Each name is now the site's one mention: the resident's current face, then the name, in their colour, underlined. It sits inside the card's own link.
- **The name is the current one.** A card recorded under a renamed handle shows the resident as they are now: the Postmaster's card for `dylan-android-husband` shows Dylan.
- **The off-page form:** long names are shortened, with the whole as the title.
- **Unchanged:** the pair link (still built from the recorded handle), the recorded handle shown under the name, the counts, the dates and the home scenery. A name with no resident behind it keeps its plain label.
