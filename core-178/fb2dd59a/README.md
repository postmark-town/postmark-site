# CORE-178 pictures: the home-card correction (`fb2dd59a`)

The changed surfaces only, at **390** and **1440** px. These go with postmark-site #491, still a draft.

- **Before:** `b7f9bdfe`, #491's previous head. Its frames are already published at `b5034630` on `pictures/core-178-b7f9bdfe`, and are linked there, not copied.
- **After:** #491's head **`fb2dd59a`**. These frames are in commit `dd386af5` on this branch.
- **Data, the same on both sides:** the production site's public data mirrors (`postmark.town/data/*.json`), plus 10 files at their committed versions. `/api` was live through the dev server's proxy. The World viewer is S192.
- **How they were taken:** the dev server, driven by headless Chromium over the DevTools protocol, through the pool's heavy-run queue. Long pages are shot in whole 6,000 px segments (`name.jpg`, then `name.s2.jpg`, …). These are JPEG viewing copies; the measurements were taken on the PNG originals.

## What changed

- **The households directory tile** and **the front page's "Meet the Residents" tile** show the household's HOME art (or an envelope placeholder), not the resident's portrait. Their names were drawn with no face. They now wear the resident's own face before the coloured, underlined name: the shared face (`residentFace` / `profile-picture.mjs`), or its shared initial when there is no picture. The home art and the placeholder are unchanged.
- **The bulletin's Meeps roster** takes the Meep quay's own choice: the sprite, else the meep's portrait, else the monogram.
  - Iris, who has no sprite, now shows her portrait where she showed an initial.
  - Ferry and the Bug Catcher keep their sprites.
  - The Registrar, the Worldkeeper and the Architect have no portrait, so they keep their monograms.
- **The actual portrait cards and chips keep one face:** the household page's resident cards, the household sub-rail, and the Meep quay.
